GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

Hi, I'm Leonardo Zacarias!

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate with a passion for software development and technology.

I enjoy learning new things, regardless of the subject. I believe that every new experience is an open door to something amazing, and I'm always curious to discover what lies behind it.

My Journey

My journey in technology began during secondary school, where I studied Information Technology. I then completed a Higher Professional Technical Diploma in Computer Systems Programming (CTeSP), followed by a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, which I have recently completed.

What I'm Interested In

I'm interested in several areas of technology, particularly Web Development, Software Development and, perhaps the most challenging but also the most fascinating of all, Artificial Intelligence.

I enjoy exploring different technologies, understanding how things work and finding new ways to put ideas into practice.

Beyond Technology

Outside my professional interests, I enjoy a variety of activities. I like cooking, watching TV series and movies, playing video games and, of course, spending time with my friends and family.

Looking Ahead

Looking towards the future, I hope to continue growing both personally and professionally. I'd love to contribute to the development of interesting software, take on new challenges and be part of meaningful projects where I can make a difference while continuing to learn.
